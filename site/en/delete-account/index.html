Delete account

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Legal entity name, address and tax ID (STIR) will be added later.

Delete in the app (driver)

  1. Open the ParkBor app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you cannot open the app, see the contact section below.

When deletion is not possible

  • You have an active or upcoming booking (awaiting payment, confirmed, vehicle on site or overstaying).
  • The account owns a parking lot. Hand it over or delete it first.
  • You are the last active platform administrator.

The app shows the reason. Try again once the conditions are met.

What is erased

  • Name, username and phone number are cleared.
  • The Telegram ID is replaced and no longer linked to the account.
  • Device push tokens are removed and sessions are revoked.
  • Operator assignments end.

What is kept

Bookings and payments are kept for accounting and legal requirements, without a link to you, in anonymised form. The notification log is deleted after 180 days.

If you wish, you can later open a new account with the same Telegram.

Contact

If you cannot delete the account in the app, write to parkbooor@gmail.com or Telegram @habibullo_2313. We may ask for your Telegram username or ID to verify it is you. Owners and operators use the same contacts.
