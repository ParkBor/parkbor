ParkBor guide

Two parts: for drivers (mobile app) and for parking lot owners (ParkBor CRM). Every step is short. The pictures are simple schematic illustrations, not real screenshots.

Download for Android

For now this is a test (beta) version for Android only. Allow “install from unknown sources” when installing. Google Play and App Store are coming soon.

A. For drivers

  1. Download and install the app

    Download the APK with the button above and open the file. Allow “install from unknown sources”. If Google Play Protect warns you, tap “More details”, then “Install anyway”.

  2. Open the app and choose a language

    Open the app. On the sign-in screen choose Uzbek, Russian or English.

  3. Sign in with Telegram

    Tap “Sign in with Telegram”. The app opens the bot @parkborbot. Press Start in Telegram. Tap “Share phone number” and share YOUR OWN number. The bot sends a code. Go back to the app and enter the 6-digit code.

  4. Enter your first name

    The app asks for your first name. Type it and tap “Save”.

  5. Add your car

    Enter the plate number with Latin letters and digits. You can add more cars later.

  6. Allow permissions

    Location is optional: it helps find lots near you. Allow notifications to get booking messages.

  7. Find a lot on the map

    The map is based on OpenStreetMap. Pick a lot and tap “Details”. Choose the slot type and the time.

  8. Pay and get your QR

    Tap “Pay and book” and pay online through the payment provider. After payment the booking QR code appears.

  9. Show the QR at the gate

    At entry show the QR to the operator. You can cancel a booking in “Bookings” with “Cancel”: the refund amount is shown first.

  10. Profile

    In “Profile” you can change the language, edit your details and tap “Delete account”. More: Delete account.

B. For lot owners (CRM)

  1. Get added as an owner

    A platform admin adds the owner by Telegram ID. Send the admin your Telegram ID. You can get it from the bot @userinfobot.

  2. Sign in to the CRM

    Open crm.parkbor.uz and sign in with Telegram.

  3. Add a parking lot

    Enter the name and pick the point on the map: the address fills in automatically. Set the working hours.

  4. Zones, slots and tariffs

    Add zones and slots (slots can be created in bulk). Then set the tariffs.

  5. The admin approves

    A platform admin approves the lot. After that drivers can see it.

  6. Invite operators

    In the CRM “Operators” section create an invitation code and send it to the operator. The operator enters it in the app: “Profile”, then “Become an operator”.

  7. Keep watch

    See bookings, payments and refunds in the CRM. Use “Export to Excel” to download data.

Troubleshooting

The code does not arrive

Make sure you pressed Start in the bot. Then request the code again in the app.

“The Telegram data is invalid or has expired” appears

Try again: restart the Telegram sign-in from the beginning.

The map is empty

Check your internet connection and reopen the app.

The site or CRM does not open

Change your DNS to 1.1.1.1, or on Android set “Private DNS” to one.one.one.one.
