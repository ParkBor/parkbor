For parking lot owners

Increase the occupancy of your parking lot

Drivers book a space in the app ahead of time and pay for it. You see spaces, income and occupancy in one place, ParkBor CRM.

Sign in to ParkBor CRMContact us

3 steps for owners

  1. Add your lot to the CRM

    Enter the number of spaces, the price and opening hours.

  2. Drivers book in advance

    A driver finds a free space in the app, books it and pays online.

  3. The operator scans the QR at the gate

    You follow income and occupancy in the CRM.

What the CRM gives you

Only features the system really has are listed here.

  • Space status

    Free, occupied and booked spaces of each lot are visible in the CRM.

  • Online payment

    The driver pays for the booking in advance through a payment provider.

  • QR control

    At entry and exit the operator scans the driver’s QR code. Codes are signed and single-use.

  • Reports and Excel

    Statistics on bookings, income and occupancy; data can be exported to Excel.

  • Operators

    You add gate staff with an invitation code. They scan QR codes in the app.

  • Telegram notifications

    Booking messages arrive through the Telegram bot and app notifications.

For drivers

Drivers use the ParkBor mobile app: find a lot nearby, book a space, pay, and show the QR code at the gate.

  • Find parking lots on a map
  • Book and pay in advance
  • QR code at entry and exit

Frequently asked questions

How do I start?

Contact us. We agree on terms and link your Telegram account as a lot owner. Then you sign in to ParkBor CRM with Telegram and add your lot.

How much does it cost?

Contact us for terms. We do not publish prices or commission here.

Who sees my data?

An owner sees bookings of their own lots only. An operator sees the booking details needed at the gate. Platform administrators have access to run the system. Details: Privacy Policy.

How does a driver pay?

The driver pays for the booking in the app on a payment provider’s page. Bank card details are not stored by ParkBor.

Do I need operators?

You can assign operators to accept bookings by QR at the gate. The owner adds an operator in the CRM with an invitation code.

How can I delete my account?

A driver opens Profile, then Delete account in the app. Details: Delete account.
